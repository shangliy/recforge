# Literature sweep 2026-10-22: what the corpus is missing

This sweep was checked against `refs.bib` (154 entries), `manual.bib`, `ids.txt`, `docs/related-work.md`, `sweep-2026-10-21.md`, `02-related-work.md` and `audit/*.md`, which together hold 159 arXiv IDs.

The cut-off is 2026-10-22. Everything below is new to the corpus unless it is marked **venue update**.

## How items were verified

- Every arXiv item: title, first author and first-version date come from the arXiv API record.
- Items marked "full text": an agent read the arXiv HTML v1 in full, with appendices. The C1–C9 quotes are verbatim, 25 words or fewer, and carry section or table pointers.
- Items marked "abstract": only the abstract was read.
- NeurIPS 2026 workshop items: the neurips.cc virtual pages were read. OpenReview put up a browser challenge, so the PDFs could not be opened. Those items are abstract-level only, and their quotes are paraphrased by the fetch tool.
- ACM venue items: the OpenAlex record (DOI, title, authors, abstract) was used.
- Code links were checked by searching each full text for GitHub URLs. **No new item releases code.**
- Affiliations are given only where the paper states them.

The codebook is the one in `audit/README.md`: C1 metric, C2 selection split, C3 test once, C4 repeats, C5 val–test gap, C6 null control, C7 budget, C8 baselines, C9 isolation, and online.

## (a) Novelty threats

**Summary: no survey or audit of code-level or config-level self-evolving recommenders was found.** The two closest pieces of work are below.

### Threat 1 (medium, survey scope): *From scoring to workflow: A survey of agentic recommender systems*

- **Bibliographic:** Yiqiao Xu, Yunqi Mi, Yingjie Wu et al. (8 authors). *Information Processing & Management* 64(2):105164, online 2026-09-14. DOI 10.1016/j.ipm.2026.105164.
- **Read depth:** abstract plus the 30 references OpenAlex resolves. ScienceDirect returned HTTP 403, so the full text was not read.
- **Scope:** 47 studies from January 2023 to April 2026, grouped into four workflow stages: User Modeling, Adaptive Decision Orchestration, Candidate Optimization and Exposure Refinement.
- **Why it is close:** it is the most recent peer-reviewed agentic-RS survey, and it discusses "feedback reliability" and "recurring failure modes".
- **Why it is not the same:**
  - It treats the agent *inside* the serving workflow, not the agent as the engineer of the recommender.
  - None of the 30 resolved references is a code- or config-evolving system. They include RAH, MMAgentRec, "Envisioning Recommendations on an LLM-Based Agent Platform" and the TSE systematic review below.
  - Its April 2026 cut-off predates most cluster-C systems.
- **Action:** cite it in the "nearest surveys" table. State the delta as: agent-in-pipeline taxonomy versus loop-that-edits-the-recommender, plus our evaluation audit. If anyone can get the PDF, confirm it does not discuss Self-EvolveRec or 2602.10226.

### Threat 2 (medium, audit angle, not a survey): *An Explicit Feedback Contract for Iterative ML-Agent Harnesses*

- **Bibliographic:** arXiv 2610.28798, 2026-10-20. Kehui Yao et al. (11 authors). Walmart Global Tech, Lehigh, Rutgers.
- **Read depth:** full text.
- **Why it is close:** it makes the survey's central methodological point experimentally, for CTR (Criteo-style) and LTR (MSLR-WEB10K).
  - The setting: exact validation feedback after every attempt opens the validation split to adaptive selection.
  - It measures the val–test gap.
  - It uses a label-permuted no-signal control.
- **Overlap with our audit:** its Table 1 classifies "Agentic recommenders (Wang et al. 2026 [2602.10226]; Zhang et al. 2026)" as lacking both bounded feedback and held-out reporting. That is a mini-audit of two of our systems.
- **Why it is not the same:** it covers two systems in one table row, has no taxonomy and no catalogue.
- **Action:** cite it prominently as concurrent, complementary evidence. Also see (b6): it can enter the audit as a methodology row.

### b1. EvoPilot: *Verify, Don't Trust: Agentic Model Development for Video Discovery Retrieval at Scale*

**Bibliographic**
- arXiv 2609.21257, 2026-09-18.
- Hao Fu, Baiting Zhu, Minglei Chen, Yinjie Huang, Shuai Ding.
- **Meta Platforms**.
- Comment: "submitted to the KDD 2027 Applied Data Science Track".
- No code. Uses Claude Opus 4.7/4.8 and Claude Haiku.
- Read depth: full text.

**What it is**
- **Level:** L1 (serving and config: interaction head, candidate depth, task-score weights) and L2 (capacity and loss retrains).
- **Signal:** offline click hit-rate@K=3000 on held-out replay, gated by a human-gated, fail-closed pair verifier, followed by one online A/B.
- **Setting:** a 37-day campaign with 7 directions, 20 rounds and 68 configurations.

**Audit codes**

| C | code | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | ranking-ns (+online) | "primary selection metric is click hit-rate at depth K" (§5.1, Eq. 4). Pool rebuilt from servable inventory; size not stated. |
| C2 | NS | One held-out replay set; no val/test distinction (§5.1). |
| C3 | NS offline; one online A/B | "We use the online A/B only to establish the product relevance of the selected model." (§5.5) |
| C4 | P | "Two publications with the same model, configuration, and pinned data differed by 0.36 points" (§5.1). The seven directions "are not independent replicates". |
| C5 | P | Offline +3.20 pp, then online +0.66% GSRR. "Offline hit-rate and online GSRR have different populations and denominators" (§5.1, Table 6). |
| C6 | N | "The primitive autoresearch baseline is a retained incident, not a concurrent randomized workflow comparison" (§6). |
| C7 | 37 days, 20 rounds, 68 configs, 1,662 launches, 97.2 h orchestrator wall-clock for 9 rounds | "we do not extrapolate token cost or GPU occupancy" (§6; Tables 4, 7). |
| C8 | production | "the baseline left the head disabled" (§3.2). |
| C9 | **gaming** (+P) | "a favorable result used an evaluation date inside the candidate model's training window, so the result was retracted" (§5.2). A date-order guard was added afterwards (Table 3). |
| online | CI, 7 days | "+0.66%, a statistically significant increase" (§5.5). |

**Reliability findings** (strong material for §6 of the survey)
- **No-op edits:** "history rewriting removed the intended edit but left a plausible revision chain" (§5.2).
- **Evaluator drift:** "the aggregate metric changed meaning" (§5.2).
- **Funnel mismatch creating a false 22-pp drop:** "the control realized (3000,3000) while the treatment realized (3000,600)" (§5.3).
- **Train/serve gap** from a seed identifier.
- **No CIs offline:** "we do not report inferential confidence intervals" (§5.1).

### b2. *AutoResearch at Production Scale: Failure Modes and a Multi-Agent Framework*

**Bibliographic**
- arXiv 2609.30541, 2026-09-24.
- Aparajith Chandran, Juwon Kim, Saurav Jha, Pablo Castells, Florian Hottier.
- **Amazon, Books Core Recs Science**.
- **IEEE ICDM 2026 (Applied Track)**.
- No code. Uses Claude Sonnet 4.6 and Opus 4.6 via Bedrock.
- Read depth: full text.

**What it is**
- Two systems: System A (book-retrieval embeddings) and System B (RQ-VAE Semantic IDs).
- **System A, L2:** the agent rewrites the whole `train.py`.
- **System B, L1 + L3:** hyperparameter and encoder-architecture edits to a **semantic-ID tokenizer**.
- **Signal:** keep-if-better.
  - System A: Recall@6, kept if it beats the best by τ.
  - System B: weighted cluster coherence.
- **Scale:** 12 weeks, 220+ experiments.

**Audit codes**

| C | code | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | A: full (FAISS over the index); B: other (unsupervised coherence) | "the six nearest neighbors … retrieved via a FAISS IVF+Flat index" (§II-A). "weighted coherence is 0.5×L1+0.3×L2+0.2×L3" (§II-B). |
| C2 | **test** | Same held-out set for keep decisions and reporting: "retains modifications that improve a held-out scalar metric" (Abstract). Results are "Recall@6 on the held-out evaluation set" (§V-B). |
| C3 | N | Headline is the trajectory peak: "1.82× relative lift … on its peak evaluation date" (§V-A). |
| C4 | P | One model re-evaluated across 11 dates: "mean 6.18% ± 0.28 pp" (§V-E). No repeated sessions. |
| C5 | P | "the peak ~2.6σ above the across-date mean"; "Even the across-date mean preserves a 1.63× lift" (§V-A/E). |
| C6 | N | "the natural campaign transitions in Table V serve as the ablation" (§V-A). |
| C7 | 220+ iterations; 9–19 h multi-GPU per iteration; 12 weeks; "~8 engineer-weeks" saved | Table I, §II-C, §VI. No total GPU-hours or tokens. |
| C8 | tuned + production | "hand-tuned baseline … product of four engineer-weeks … 3.79% Recall@6" (§II-A). Deployed baseline 2.82%. |
| C9 | **gaming** (+P: evaluator immutable) | "maximizing codebook size trivially improves weighted coherence … the resulting hierarchy was unusable" (§III-E). |
| online | none | "online pilots … are underway" (§VI). |

**Reliability findings**
- Five named failure modes: infrastructure fragility, agent memory decay, search-direction stagnation, iteration-cost asymmetry, **metric fixation**.
- "the agent treats the metric as ground truth" (§III-E).
- **Index-coverage confound** (sub-agent's inference, not stated by the authors): an agent-added text fallback grew FAISS coverage from about 17% to the full catalogue. The candidate set behind Recall@6 therefore changed mid-campaign.
- **Note:** this is the first audited system that evolves a semantic-ID tokenizer, an L3 component, in production.

### b3. PILOT: *PILOT Technical Report* (Proactive Insight Learner for Online Tree-Experiments)

**Bibliographic**
- arXiv 2608.18637, v1 2026-08-19.
- "PILOT Team", 20 authors in alphabetical order. Core contributors: Jiuning Lin, Ruiquan Lan, Xiaodong Zhu.
- **Taobao & Tmall Group of Alibaba**, per the deployment text and an internship footnote.
- 42-page technical report. No code.
- Read depth: full text.

**What it is**
- **Level:** L1. Per-bucket 8-dimensional ranking-objective weight vectors, wrapped in user-segment PolicyTrees.
- **Proposers:** LLM roles (Manager, Search Planner, Memory Curator). They choose only from a deterministic "legal-command envelope".
- **Memory (L4-like):** a strategy and methodology store.
- **Signal:** online whole-bucket A/B against the Champion, with pre-registered criteria.
- **ROAM**, the comparison agent, is defined only inside this report. No separate paper was found on arXiv.

**Audit codes**

| C | code | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | online | "IPV is the north-star optimization target. PV and transaction count serve as critical guardrails" (§6.1). |
| C2 | online | "adoption requires a statistically conclusive whole-bucket comparison against the Champion" (§4.3). |
| C3 | na. Designed Y, but not reported | "Final confirmation evaluates the Champion against a single pre-registered test … The test runs once." (§3.4). No confirmation result in §6. |
| C4 | N | "We report the first five days." (§6.1). The 5 buckets are parallel arms. |
| C5 | P (designed, not reported) | "The data collected while searching … shaped the choice of Champion, so it cannot also serve as proof." (§3.4) |
| C6 | N | ROAM is also an LLM agent. A deterministic-only cell is planned but not run (§6.3.1, Table 8). |
| C7 | buckets × days | "Five experiment buckets (21–25) … +1.0% IPV within 7 days" (§6.1). Budget is in "bucket-days" (§4.1). |
| C8 | production + seed (in-house ROAM) | Fixed baseline bucket B0 (§6.1). |
| C9 | P | "LLM roles only choose among options these services already generated: they cannot invent a command, alter a statistical conclusion" (§2). |
| online | 5 days reported; no CI | Lifts are point estimates (Tables 7, 9). |

**Reliability findings**
- "The comparison in §6.2 is before/after, not randomized." (§6.3.1). Also: "suggestive rather than causal."
- The headline "+1.40% IPV" is a best-of-5-bucket maximum.
- Sub-agent reading: the top buckets 22 and 23 were "untouched" by the loop (Table 7).
- **Good-practice design to credit as stated design:** pre-registration, a single confirmation sample, and a rule forbidding "almost significant" readings. This is the only system in the corpus with a stated pre-registered test-once rule.

### b8. *MLE Agents: Autonomous ML Engineering in Production Recommendation Systems* (abstract only)

**Bibliographic**
- NeurIPS 2026 **Workshop for Autonomous Machine Learning Research**, oral.
- Matthew Li, Albert J. Liang, Dilip Guru Raj. Affiliation not shown.
- OpenReview `b2UcmSJTPK`; neurips.cc/virtual/2026/157618.
- No arXiv version found.

**From the abstract (paraphrased)**
- **Level:** L2. Agents write model changes as code for production search rankers serving more than 1B requests per day.
- **Selection:** the agent reads offline evaluation and keeps or discards each change, with humans on the loop.
- **Scale:** 1,097 completed candidates, 325 accepted. The candidate win rate rose from 15.6% to 29.6%.
- **Online:** an A/B on about 10M users gave CTR +0.70% and search volume +0.23%.
- **Stated difficulties:** "offline metrics don't always forecast online effects", hours-long feedback, plateauing gains.
- **Notable for C5:** the reported acceptance rate (325 of 1,097) is the largest keep-count in the corpus. It invites a winner's-curse analysis.
- **Action:** add to the borderline list and retrieve the PDF when OpenReview is reachable.
